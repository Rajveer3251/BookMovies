# BookMovies demo recording script (about 90 seconds)

1. Open the deployed frontend and show the landing page and movie cards.
2. Search for a movie by title or genre.
3. Click **Book tickets**, choose a showtime, and show the seat map.
4. Register a test account (use a disposable demo email) or sign in.
5. Select two available seats and confirm the booking.
6. Open **My bookings** and show the confirmed seats and total.
7. Briefly show the backend `/api/health` endpoint returning `{"status":"ok","service":"BookMovies API"}`.

Suggested title: `BookMovies | Full-Stack Movie Ticket Booking App Demo`

Do not record real passwords, personal data, or secret environment values. Upload the finished recording to YouTube as Unlisted (or another host) and paste its share URL into the project README.
